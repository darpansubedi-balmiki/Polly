PRODUCT An AI-powered English proficiency coaching web app for IELTS Academic, IELTS General and PTE Academic. Not a question bank — an AI coach. Core promise, visible across the product: "Know your score. Know why. Know what to do next." Three pillars in priority order: realistic exam simulation, explainable AI scoring, personalized daily coaching. Every UI element must answer one of: What should I do now? Where am I? How am I performing? Why did I get this score? What should I improve? What should I practice next? How close am I to my target? Am I ready for the exam? If it answers none, remove it.  USERS Adults 18–35 preparing for university, immigration or work visas. Under financial and time pressure; a retake costs money and delays life plans. Emotional state: exam anxiety, fear of an unpredictable score, uncertainty about what to study, inconsistent motivation. Design response: familiarity reduces anxiety, explainability reduces uncertainty, one recommended action reduces decision fatigue, visible trajectory builds confidence. DUAL-MODE SYSTEM — THE SIGNATURE IDEA Two visually distinct environments sharing ONE component library.  COACH MODE (dashboard, practice, feedback, study plan, analytics, marketing): Dark, focused, premium. Canvas #0B132B, surfaces #111A34 and #16203D, hairline borders rgba(255,255,255,0.08), radius 12, card padding 24, 32–40px between major sections. Two elevation levels only: Rest (border only) and Raised (0 8px 24px rgba(0,0,0,.35)). Polly the parrot mascot present. Streaks, XP and badges visible. Feels like a serious training facility, not a game.  EXAM MODE (full mock tests only): A deliberate visual downgrade to institutional neutrality. Canvas #FFFFFF, panels #F4F5F7, borders 1px #D5D8DE, text #1A1D23. Typography switches to a neutral system-grade sans (Arial/Helvetica-like) at 16px/1.6 to mirror real exam software. Radius 4. NO shadows, NO gradients, NO illustrations, NO brand chrome. The only mint pixel permitted is the active recording indicator or the selected-answer fill. Polly, streaks, XP, badges, notifications and the app sidebar are entirely removed — the exam takes the full viewport. The contrast between these two modes is the product's signature design decision. Make it unmistakable.  TYPOGRAPHY (Coach Mode) Display/headings: Inter Tight or Satoshi — H1 40/48 semibold, H2 30/38 semibold, H3 22/30 medium, H4 18/26 medium. Body: Inter — Body-L 16/26, Body 15/24, Body-S 13/20, Caption 12/16 uppercase tracking 0.04em. All scores, bands, timers and counters use tabular-lining figures. Score Display style: 56/56 semibold tabular — score numbers must never reflow or shift width. Icons: 1.5px stroke, 20px and 24px, rounded joins, monochrome. No mixing filled and outline styles. Motion: functional and calm. 150–200ms ease-out for state changes, 300ms count-up for score reveal. No parallax, no decorative looping animation.  POLLY — THE AI COACH MASCOT A parrot rendered as a modern, intelligent tutor: clean geometric flat vector in navy and mint with subtle depth. Explicitly NOT childish — no oversized cartoon eyes, no bouncing, no exclamation-heavy speech bubbles. Closer to a considered fintech brand character than a kids' app. States: Idle, Listening (subtle waveform ring), Analyzing, Explaining, Celebrating (restrained). Polly appears ONLY in Coach Mode. Practice/Learning Mode → Polly active, hosts the "Squawk Box" error log. Mock Test Simulator Mode → Polly entirely hidden, zero gamification.  CONTENT RULES Realistic content everywhere. No Lorem ipsum, no "Card title", no "Description here", no "Sample text". Use authentic exam terminology: Read Aloud, Repeat Sentence, Describe Image, Retell Lecture, Answer Short Question, Summarize Written Text, Write Essay, Reading & Writing Fill in the Blanks, Re-order Paragraphs, Highlight Incorrect Words, Write from Dictation, Summarize Spoken Text, Summarize Group Discussion, Respond to a Situation; IELTS: Task 1, Task 2, Cue Card, True/False/Not Given, Matching Headings, Fluency & Coherence, Lexical Resource, Grammatical Range & Accuracy, Pronunciation. Plausible values only: IELTS bands 0.0–9.0 in 0.5 steps; PTE scores 10–90; raw scores out of 40. Persona: Aayush Sharma, target PTE 79, currently predicted 73, exam in 42 days.  ACCESSIBILITY WCAG AA minimum (4.5:1 body, 3:1 large text). Never encode meaning in color alone — pair every status with an icon, sign or text label. The four skill colors must stay distinguishable in greyscale (vary lightness, not only hue). Minimum 44×44px targets. Visible focus rings. Charts use direct data labels, not legend-only.  DO NOT No generic SaaS or LMS dashboard. No symmetrical grid of equal-weight KPI tiles. No purple/violet "AI" gradients. No glassmorphism. No gradient meshes or blurred color blobs. No 24px+ radius on everything. No childish education aesthetics or confetti storms. No stock photography. No mint spread across large surfaces. Polly never appears in Exam Mode. The exam simulator never carries brand styling. Never prioritize visual impressiveness over the user knowing what to do next.
