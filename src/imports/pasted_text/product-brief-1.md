PRODUCT
An AI-powered English proficiency coaching web app for IELTS Academic, IELTS General and PTE
Academic. Not a test bank — an AI coach that makes serious exam prep feel like a modern consumer
product. Core promise, visible across the product: "Know your score. Know why. Know what to do
next." Three pillars in priority order: realistic exam simulation, explainable AI scoring,
personalized daily coaching delivered through an engaging, low-friction experience.

Guiding principle for every screen: LESS FRICTION, MORE PROGRESS. The user should never have to
think "what am I supposed to do next?" Every element answers one of: What should I do now?
Where am I? How am I progressing? How close am I to my goal? Why did I get this score? What should
I improve next? If an element answers none of these, remove it.

USERS
Adults 18–35 preparing for university, immigration or work visas. Under financial and time
pressure — a retake costs money and delays life plans. Emotional state: exam anxiety, fear of an
unpredictable score, uncertainty about what to study, inconsistent motivation.
Design response: familiarity reduces anxiety, explainability reduces uncertainty, one recommended
action reduces decision fatigue, visible trajectory and delight build confidence and return visits.

★★★ TWO-MODE SYSTEM — THE SIGNATURE PRODUCT IDEA ★★★
This product has two deliberately different visual worlds sharing one underlying component
library and token system. This split must be unmistakable — never let the two visual languages
bleed into each other.

── COACH MODE (marketing, onboarding, dashboard, practice, feedback, study plan, progress,
   achievements — everything except an active full mock test) ──
A warm, modern, consumer-product feel inspired by best-in-class learning apps: friendly,
motivating, high-energy, but never childish or cluttered. Characteristics:
  - Rounded geometry: 16–20px card radius, 999px pill radius on badges/chips/CTAs.
  - Soft, layered depth: gentle shadows, no harsh flat dark panels everywhere — mix dark navy
    surfaces with occasional light/cream card surfaces (#F7F5EF or similar warm neutral) for
    variety and warmth, similar to how modern learning apps alternate dark chrome with bright
    content cards.
  - Illustration-forward: use simple, modern vector illustrations and Polly the parrot generously
    on marketing, onboarding, empty states, celebration moments. Illustration style: flat geometric
    vector, bold clean shapes, limited palette (navy, mint, warm neutral, one or two supporting
    accent hues), confident and friendly — NOT a corporate stock-illustration style, NOT
    cutesy/childish either. Think "confident modern app," not "kids' education portal."
  - Generous whitespace and large, confident typography — headlines should feel like a product
    announcement, not a form label.
  - Motion matters here: celebratory micro-animations on completion (subtle confetti-free
    "pop"/scale/glow), smooth step transitions, progress bars that animate fill, badges that
    unlock with a satisfying reveal. Motion is tasteful and quick (150–400ms), never gimmicky or
    looping.
  - Copy tone: encouraging and human. "Great start! 🔥" "You're getting better." "One more
    practice to reach your goal." "New personal best!" Never patronizing, never overly cute.

── EXAM MODE (a full, timed Mock Test only — Listening/Reading/Writing/Speaking simulation
   screens while an official mock is in progress) ──
A hard, deliberate visual downgrade to institutional neutrality. This is non-negotiable and must
stay exactly as serious as real exam software, REGARDLESS of how playful Coach Mode becomes.
Canvas #FFFFFF, panels #F4F5F7, borders 1px #D5D8DE, text #1A1D23. Neutral system-grade sans
(Arial/Helvetica-like), 16px/1.6. Radius 4px. NO shadows, NO gradients, NO illustrations, NO
rounded playful chrome, NO Polly, NO XP/streak UI, NO celebratory motion. The only mint pixel
permitted is the active recording indicator or the selected-answer fill. Zero gamification —
this is an explicit, stated client requirement, not a stylistic choice, and must never be
softened even though the rest of the product is warm and playful.
The contrast between these two modes IS the product's signature UX idea: entering a mock test
should feel like walking into an exam hall after being in a friendly practice studio.

TYPOGRAPHY (Coach Mode)
Headings: a confident, slightly rounded modern sans (e.g. Inter Tight, or a geometric rounded
family) — H1 44/52 bold, H2 32/40 semibold, H3 24/32 semibold, H4 18/26 medium.
Body: Inter — Body-L 16/26, Body 15/24, Body-S 13/20, Caption 12/16.
All scores, XP, timers and counters use tabular-lining figures and must never reflow width.
Icons: rounded-corner, 1.5–2px stroke or friendly filled style, 20/24px, monochrome unless it's a
celebratory/achievement icon (those may use full color).
Exam Mode typography: switches entirely to the neutral system sans defined above — do not carry
Coach Mode type styles into Exam Mode.

POLLY — THE AI COACH MASCOT
A parrot as a modern, friendly guide character — clean flat-vector geometry in navy/mint/warm
neutral tones, expressive but not babyish. Confident and warm, like a good tutor, not a cartoon
sidekick. States: Idle, Listening (waveform ring), Analyzing (thinking), Explaining (pointing at
feedback), Celebrating (a genuine, warm celebration — not exaggerated).
Polly appears throughout Coach Mode: homepage hero, onboarding, dashboard greeting, practice
screens, feedback review ("Squawk Box" error callouts), idle reminders, achievement unlocks.
Polly is 100% ABSENT from Exam Mode — no exceptions.

GAMIFICATION SYSTEM (Coach Mode / Practice only — never inside a scored Mock Test)
Core mechanics, each with a clear UX purpose (never decorative-only):
  - XP: earned per completed practice item, visible as an animating XP bar with level-ups.
  - Streaks: daily practice streak with a flame indicator and a "protect your streak" nudge.
  - Badges/Achievements: unlocked for milestones (first mock test, 7-day streak, skill mastery).
  - Progress bars & question counters: inside practice sessions, always visible, always accurate.
  - Celebration states: short, tasteful animation + encouraging microcopy on completion, personal
    bests, and streak milestones.
Gamification never appears inside a live scored Mock Test — it resumes immediately after the
Mock Test Result Summary screen, which sits back in Coach Mode.

CONTENT RULES
Realistic content everywhere. No Lorem ipsum, no "Card title," no "Description here." Use real
exam terminology: Read Aloud, Repeat Sentence, Describe Image, Retell Lecture, Answer Short
Question, Summarize Written Text, Write Essay, Reading & Writing Fill in the Blanks, Re-order
Paragraphs, Highlight Incorrect Words, Write from Dictation, Summarize Spoken Text, Summarize
Group Discussion, Respond to a Situation; IELTS: Task 1, Task 2, Cue Card, True/False/Not Given,
Matching Headings, Fluency & Coherence, Lexical Resource, Grammatical Range & Accuracy,
Pronunciation. Plausible values: IELTS bands 0.0–9.0 in 0.5 steps; PTE 10–90; raw scores /40.
Persona: Aayush Sharma, target PTE 79, currently predicted 73, exam in 42 days, on a 12-day streak.

ACCESSIBILITY
WCAG AA minimum (4.5:1 body text, 3:1 large text). Never encode meaning in color alone — pair
every status with an icon, sign, or label. Skill colors distinguishable in greyscale. Minimum
44×44px touch targets. Visible focus rings. Charts carry direct data labels. Exam Mode stays fully
legible for long reading sessions without eye strain.

DO NOT
No generic SaaS/LMS dashboard. No symmetrical grid of equal-weight KPI tiles. No purple/violet
"AI" gradients. No glassmorphism, gradient meshes, or blurred color blobs. No stock photography.
No mint spread across large surfaces. Polly never appears in Exam Mode. Exam Mode never receives
brand chrome, rounded playful styling, or any gamification — this rule overrides the general
"make it fun" direction and must hold even under later iteration pressure. Never let visual
impressiveness outrank the user knowing what to do next.
